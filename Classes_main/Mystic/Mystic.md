# Core class

## telepathy
One of the first techiques a mystic learns is how to communicate through thought alone. provided you have line of sight with your target creature(s), you may speak with them telepathically.
- the range of your telepathy is equal to a number of metres equal to your sense score.
- if the target creature is hostile or neutral, they may react with surprise or suspicion, as per a *message* aspect. 
- telepathic speech will wake a sleeping creature. 


## psionics
Psionics are the core of your mental training. Psionics allow you to access **psionic diciplines**, and deploy them, at the cost of **psi points**.

your psionics power is mainly comprised of the following features: **psi points, mystic recovery, psionic diciplines and silent psionics**.

### Silent psionics
Psionics is silent and motionless, meaning all disciplines on the mystic's aspect list are considered *psionic disciplines* for you. They **do not require any components to manifest**. if a discipline otherwise has a material cost, as long as the material is on your character, **you are not required to hold it.**

### psi points
Psi points represent the total amount of mental energy you can harness to channel into your psychic power. These are consumed via psionic diciplines and psionic philosophies.

**regaining psi points**: After a respite, you regain all of your psi points. 

### psionic diciplines
A psionic dicipline is a specific methodology or technique in psionic practice you can prepare. *psychic knife*, *ward*, and *Sense lie* are reccomended.

**learning aspects**: at level 0, you select any two diciplines of your choice of Tier 1 from the mystic aspect list. As you gain levels in the mystic class, you will continue to gain more slots to prepare more aspects.

**known aspects**. Mystics memorise their diciplines through rigorous practice, and it remains in their memory until changed. 

**changing known diciplines**: You may change your known aspects on a level up or a recovery. Each time you change your known aspects, you may also change one of your **psionic talents** as detailed below.

**psionic diciplines are not spells.** They are considered magical effects, but not spells. For example, counterspell cannot be used against a dicipline, but they will be supressed under an antimagic field.

# milestone 1

## Mystic philosophies 
Mystics have a unique method of selecting class features. As shown in the *avaliable philosophies* column in your class block, Mystics have a wealth of different class features - *philosophies* avaliable to select. 

Each philosophy can be temporarily further enhanced to grant more boons, through allocating a number of experience points to each, allowing you to select which features are most deserving of the upgrade. 

However, in return for this flexibility, mystics have a limited number of philosophies they can prepare, being the number of *philosophy slots* you have at a given level, as shown in the *philosophy slots* column in your class block.

### Focus points
For mystics, preparing philosophies does **not** cost you any experience points. 

Instead, since experience points are not used to aquire a philosophy like a normal class feature, they are instead used to temporarily boost an already-prepared philosophy. 

In the context of philosophies, your experience pool is converted to *focus points*.

Each philosophy has a number of *tiers* - each further tier costing more and more focus points to access. You can add and remove Focus points from a philosophy after a respite.

#### preparing philosophies

You can prepare a number of philosophies equal to the number of philosophy slots you have. Upon doing so, the philosophy is considered prepared, and you may now access its effects and abilities.

You may change your chosen prepared philosophies after a Rest.

#### psychic focus
When one of your philosophies has more focus points added than any others prepared, it becomes your *psychic focus*, which enhances its features.

# Milestone feature options

## milestone 1
### mystic recovery
When outside of initiative, you spend one minute in restorative meditation, which restores all of your psi points. when you do so, you cannot take another psychic recovery until you finish a rest. 

### Cold cognition
You can no longer recieve the *charmed* or *frightened* condition by **nonmagical** means.

You ignore any any further effects, damage, or checks / saving throws of an effect that rely on having those conditions (e.g, the mental illusion of *phantasmal force*)
