# Mystic
mystics are philosophers of the dark. Sometimes solitary, often mysterious, Mystics devote themselves to a serene reverence: The night sky, the Dark side of the moon, a veil of magic, a shadow cast - The dark is what they ponder. From blades shaped from shadows to formless nanoblack, Mystics are one with the endless night.

### reverence
Mystics approach darkness not as a lack - but a necessary absense. In the noise of magic within the world, Mystics often seek harmony, serenity, or stillness within it all, regardless if that goal costs a human life or a minute of theirs. Mystics are unified through this pursuit - some feverishly fantatic, some scholarly, some without emotion. 

### Philosophy of dark
Mystics constantly shape darkness to their will - not with brute force, but to wax and wane with it until they meld with every edge. They have an endless list of philosophies - "forms" for the dark to take at their will. But when brute force is the answer - their shadow knife can pierce physical form and sever the fire of life. For that reason, their power originates from their aspects of dark as much as their core class itself, merely two philosophies of the same abyss.

### assassins and astronomers
Mystics can be profoundly indifferent to human emotion and trouble, as they sink deeper into the depths of tranquil nothingness. This leads them into excellent, traditional Roguish roles, or into their own curiosity to what lies *within* the dark. These are just a few of the subclass options your Mystic cahracter can choose from upon reaching level 2.

### Hide in plain sight
You can automatically succeed on the stealth action if you are within:
- darkness, magical or not
- full cover, or any half cover of natural foliage

you are also considered **invisible** to any magical sensors or divination, though other senses like tremorsense or truesight can still detect you.

### shadow knife
Considered a philosophy of dark, a shadow knife is a Mystic's simplest strategy for a lethal blow. The Mystic can fabricate the weapon from whisps of darkness.<br> 

The Shadow knife is a one handed, light weapon with the following properties:

- it weighs nothing, sells for nothing, and is functionally nonexistant outside of attacking.
- The shadow knife deals 1d6 + your finesse modifier in PATK damage.
- the shadow knife can benefit from any aspect or class talent that targets general weapons or light weapons.


#### manifest and Dispel
The Mystic can manifest or Dispel a shadow knife with 1AP, provided they are **within melee of darkness.**

## The pierce action
The Mystic's shadow knife, under certain conditions, can pierce physical and magical defense alike, rendering them significantly weaker than their actual protection. <br>
1. You may take the pierce action once per turn with *any proficient, non-heavy weapon*, costing 2AP. It counts as an attack action, but forbades you from taking **further attack actions** that turn. 
<br><br>
2. The **Pierce** action is avaliable when attacking whilst *hidden* or *invisible*, or against *surprised* creatures. If you lose one of those conditions, but another remains, you can still take the Pierce action.

### Sneak attack
When you successfully land a **pierce** PATK roll, you deal an additional 1d8 to your damage die.
<br><bR>
This is further upgraded when you gain the *piercing dark*  feature.

## piercing dark
A piercing dark is a silent, efficient killer, one that crosses through the physical barrier and into the metaphysical. it harms the soul and strikes a bitter cold into the burning fire of life.

The *pierce* class feature additionally gains the **piercing dark** boon.
If the Mystic fulfills one or multiple of many *piercing dark* conditions, they can take the **Pierce** attack action.

The action can be taken as a *PATK* roll with any light or simple weapon. <br>
or an *AATK* roll still with any light or simple weapon, and additionally, **any invocation that deals damage.**

### piercing dark state
This is a **state** the Mystic enters when they meet the conditions for it. If they meet multiple conditions, the piercing dark state **gains a stack** per condition met. Piercing dark state can gain stacks from other methods too. <br>
- The maximum number of stacks is equal to your **Tier limit**. 
- You lose one stack at the end of each turn. You additionally lose a stack for taking the Pierce action.
- You exist the piercing dark state when you lose all stacks, or are forced into a *restrained* condition.

#### gaining stacks - meeting conditions.
If the mystic is:
- hidden: +1
- invisible: +2
- within melee of the target (unnoticed): +1

if the target (creature) is:
- prone: +1
- surprised: +2
- restrained: +2

...more conditions, and the number of stacks they grant, can be accessed through taking *philosophies of dark*.

### Severing attack
an upgraded form of *sneak attack*. When you land a pierce PATK / AATK, you roll an additional amount of **d8s** equal to the stacks of Piercing dark you had. The damage of these die correspond with the damage of the weapon / cantrip used to take the Pierce action.

#### Strikethrough pierce
if a PATK or AATK roll hits multiple targets, as long as they're within *melee* range of each other, or occupy the same space, they all take the additional *pierce* damage. However, this consumes an equal number of tiers of Piercing dark.

## invocations
Mystics do not cast spells; their aspects are *invocations*, which they invoke with their dark shards as exchange. Invocations are esoteric methods of power, often too complex and erratic for a traditional spell. Your invocations gives you moments of profound, if violent, power.

#### learning invocations
As a mystic, when you gain the invocation feature, you learn *three* aspects of Tier 1. 
- On each odd level after, you may learn two additional aspects from the *Mystic invocations* list. They must be of a tier equal to or lower than your **tier limit**.
- On an even level, you gain one aspect this way.

once an incanation is learned, it becomes bound to you, and you cannot change them like a prepared aspect, without the following:

#### changing aspects
- On a level up: you may exchange all your aspect choices with any other aspects on the Mystic invocations* list. They must be of a tier equal to or lower than your **tier limit**.
- on a recovery period: you may exchange a number of aspects equal to your *experience* modifier.

In both cases, your total number of aspects **must be equal to the amount you had before exchanging aspects** this way. This is accounted for before learning new aspects.

### dark shards
Mystics are serene, but they recognise the time for decisive, if brutal action. You have a number of Dark shards dictated by your class block. These are concentrated, frozen fragments of darkness that can unleash powerful *invocations*.

#### dark shard Tier
your Dark shards are inert until activated, yet they have a degree of latent potency - their Tier. The tier of your dark shards is always equal to your **tier limit**.

#### upcasted invocations
when you invoke an invocation, you must expend a dark shard. The Aspect is automatically activated at the Tier of your **tier limit**, if it can be upcast. 

### piercing dark enhancement
you can instantly expend a dark shard to enter the *piercing dark* state - at a tier equal to your dark shard. 

# Milestone 2

### nanoblack
The mystic surrenders their form, their mortal husk, to the dark. They assume a *nanoblack* state - as they dissassemble into a shadow. They can move independantly in dim light or darkness, or assume the shadow of another target.

- Shadow motion: you can climb or swim across any surface with darkness upon it.
- assume shadow: whilst you're within melee of another shadow, you can assume it's place, being moved with whereever the target moves to or is being moved to. Motion this way costs **no movement speed**.
- enhanced motion: in the nanoblack state, your Movement speed is increased by a number of meters equal to your **finesse** modifier, which is independant of the generic movement speed increase of finesse.

#### entering and leaving
Entering the nanoblack state requires 2AP as a special action. leaving costs none. You can sustain the nanoblack state for a number of turns equal to your **level**.

#### nanoblack accuracy
when in the nanoblack state, you are constantly considered to be in the *piercing dark* state, at maximum stacks continuously until you exist the nanoblack state.

### evasion
Select one of your traits as your evasion trait. When subject to a saving throw with that trait, you take half damage on a failed save, and no damage on a sucessful save. This only applies to MATK and PATK saving throws.
