# wizard
Wizards are the height of mortal magic. They explore the intricate threads of arcane magic, weaving delicate rituals to explosive spells. From frigid ice, crackling lightning, sizzling acid, to planar portals, magic circles, levitation, brute force mind control, and more. Wizards are masters of force and energy. 

### A scholar's duty
Wizards are fundamentally scholars - regardless if they're enrolled in arcane academia. They delve into everything from ancient rituals of eldritch runes to modern, elegant constructions of precise geometric spell structures. Some come in to seek the deepest secrets of magic. Some come in seeking power that rivals steel and gunpowder. All respect and revel in the near divine elegance that is arcane magic, for without it, there would be no other forms of magic.

### pristine spellcasting
All wizards share one unified type of aspects - spells. Spellcasting defines a wizard as much as their wand or pointy hat. It's the core of their class, and all of a wizard's class features come together to enhance their spellcasting. The wizard has the largest spell list - if not, largest aspect list, out of any class, offering no lack of options at all - from the whimsical and wonderous to the earth-shattering.

### the Blue Mage
The term wizard is a modern invention. In the ancient era of blue divinity, wizards were Blue mages, capable of manipulating the blue-tinged colour of mana that seeped from ley lines. Even to this day, Wizards rely on this mysterious substance for sustained spellcasting or to brave intricate, high-tier spells; However, their own internal soul is more than enough to sustain most of their magical needs.

### schools of wizardry
Wizards often pursue specific branches of magical thought and expertise as they further their learning. the specific schools vary from different arcane academia across the world. For your PC, these are the subclass options your wizard character can choose from upon hitting milestone 1.

### spellcasting
The Wizard has the standard spellcasting model. [[Wizard spell list]]

#### prepared spells
Though you have collected a broad list of known spells, you can only have a number of them prepared and active at one time. you prepare a number of spells that have a total cost of **your wizard level**.

#### known spells
As a Wizard, you begin this class with your choice from six spells of Tier 1, and two of Tier 2 from the Wizard spell list.

### Magical focus
you can channel your magical power through an external focus. The most common option is a grimoire. Though there is no limit to the number of physical objects you can carry (other than your physical ability), you can only attune with one focus at a time, and it becomes your magical focus and gains the *attuned* and *magical* properties. Over the course of a recovery, you may choose to attune to a different focus.
<Br><Br>
you can scribe a *certain number of spells* of a certain maximum aspect tier onto each focus, determined by its rarity. For a spellbook, this might be calligraphing with magical ink. For a wand or staff, this might be intricately carving in runes, an orb may need you to etch in your incantations. these are now known as **inscriptions**, and they remain until the item is destroyed or the magic is removed, not supressed.

#### Inscriptions
when wielding a magical focus you are attuned to, any inscriptions are temporarily considered *known spells* that you can prepare as part of your spell list. These are automatically removed from your spell list and known spells when you unnatune from the magical focus, or it is removed from your person.

### signiature spells
refer to the signiature spells column on the class block. Signiature spells are spells so thoroughly mastered, you can cast them with minimal effort or for maximum potency. When you cast a signiature spell, you gain the ability to cast it with specific boons, such as with *shadow mana* instead of mana. 
- You select your signiature spells from your list of known spells. 
- The number of signiature spells you can prepare is equal to your **lore modifier**. 
-  To change your options for a signiature spell, you must finish a recovery.

# MILESTONE 2
### modify spell
As a Wizard, your extensive study of magic has allowed you to deconstruct a spell and modify its components. you can prepare a number of spell modifications through your aspect system, each tiered by its potency. 
<Br><Br>
1. **you can modify one spell per turn**. When you cast a spell, you can  choose to apply one of your current prepared modifications to that spell at the cost of a *special* action.
<Br><Br>
2. **a spell can only be modified once**. When you apply a modify spell talent, the spell retains that modification and cannot reciever further modifications.

you always have the following options prepared:
- Increase its range by half its original range, if it has one. If it has no range, it becomes a melee range spell.
- reroll a number of die on the spell effect equal to your lore modifier.

#### singular modification
A modified spell retains its appropriate modification until until you finish a **respite**, after which it reverts. Modifying a spell of tier 8 or higher requires you to finish a **Rest** before it reverts.

### signiature spell enhancement
When the mana cost of a signiature spell reaches a number equal to or below tier 2, you may cast it at will.

### contingency spell
A Wizard is always prepared for any magical occasion. You gain access to*contingency condition* talents. A contingency condition is a specific trigger condition, that when met, instantly casts a "contingency spell" of your choice.
<br><br>
A contingency condition can be bound to **any known spell** that you currently have to become the contingency spell. When you select said spell, you expend the mana cost upfront. 
<br><br>
Contingency conditions remain dormant until you complete a recovery, in which they must either be rebound to new contingency spells, or you select other talents.
